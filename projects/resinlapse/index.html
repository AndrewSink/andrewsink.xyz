ResinLapse

The easiest way to make timelapse videos with your resin 3D printer.

ResinLapse is a specially designed sensor that allows DSLR cameras to capture resin 3D prints and allow users to make smooth timelapse videos. Designed in collaboration with YouTuber Uncle Jessy, ResinLapse was originally launched in 2021. I was responsible for the development of the sensor assembly, manufacturing, and packaging of the finished product.

Resinlapse launched with a solution for Canon DSLR cameras, but later expanded to cover Nikon and others with a total of over 1,500 units sold. In addition to direct sales, I actively managed distribution with partners in Taiwan and Europe.
