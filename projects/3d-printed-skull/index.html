3D Printed Skull from CT Scan

A 1:1 scale model of my skull, created from CT scan DICOM data.

Using medical imaging DICOM data from a CT scan, I segmented, prepared, and 3D printed a 1:1 scale model of my own skull. The model is also available to download and print.
